The Runway

A no-calculus track toward the half turn: each page is one idea, one interactive object, and nothing you have to take on faith. Read them in order; each earns the next.

  1. Step 1
    Return Before Radius

    Three shapes, one track: "round" defined by coming back, before any mention of a radius.

  2. Step 1b
    Where Round Comes From

    Reachable sets: run every allowed move and watch the circle appear as a consequence, not a definition.

  3. Step 1c
    Axes Are Moves

    An axis is not a place, it is something you do — moves, mirrors, and what composing them buys.

  4. Step 1d
    Reach in Three Dimensions

    The reach of moves in 3D, its shadow back in 2D, and the bridge table the next step will stand on.

  5. Step 2
    The Half Turn

    Arrows that cancel, squares that make chances, and the loop that flips what it carries — the doorway to amplitudes and the half-steps of superconductivity.

The open question this track is waiting on: where does step 2 break for you — the arrows, the squares, or the seam? Step 3 (why squares, of all rules) is built from that answer.
