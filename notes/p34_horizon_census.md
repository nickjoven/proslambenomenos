# P-34: the horizon-coincidence census

## What was asked

A-8: the horizon-scale coincidences each carry a literature of
proposed mechanisms. Price them all against one derived codebook
and report the bits, not the vibes.

## The instrument (AGENTS item 8, applied)

- **Detector null (8a)**: the coverage density of codebook-
  expressible numbers, by exact interval union: p(0.05 dex) =
  0.176, p(0.1) = 0.275, p(0.3) = 0.374. Factor-two matches are
  cheap by derivation, before any entry is examined.
- **Conservation identity (8b)**: {ħ, c, G, H0} has a rank-3
  dimension matrix, so every coincidence is a (k, prefactor) pair
  in μ = H0·t_P = 1.178e-61. Slots are few by algebra.
- **Domain (8c)**: lattice and prefactors declared; the 10.155-dex
  cell verified to keep adjacent prefactor clouds disjoint.
- **Calibration**: the Gibbons-Hawking row (an equation) at
  < 1e-9 dex, excluded from counting.

The two most famous horizon coincidences are worth nothing; the
best is worth two bits; none reaches the 3-bit mechanism-line
flag. And the k = 2 density slot is claimed by four named
mechanisms at once (CKN, Sorkin, Zeldovich, holographic DE) —
they cannot all be right, and the slot they share was cheap.

error: the band was set below the instrument's own derivable
sensitivity — the pinned coverage slope gives ~40 bits/dex near

motion was computable in advance from the registration's own
numbers. Item 8a, one derivative up. Substance unaffected (a0
spans −0.03 to +1.03 bits across the H0 pair).
