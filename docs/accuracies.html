Five Kinds of Zero

Every number on the ledger carries an accuracy, and the accuracy is a claim about the instrument, not the physics. This board sorts the earned frontier's numbers — 0.0, 2×10−12, 3.6×10−5, 2.3×10−4, 10−6 — into their five species, and recomputes the specimens live on this board. I.The taxonomy

The species determines the verdict: an "error" of 10−13 is a catastrophe in species one, business as usual in species two, and invisible in species five. The ledger's discipline is to say which species every number belongs to.
