## The three rungs, each a backed statement

1. UNTENDED PHASE - tau1 = 1/D exactly. cos theta is the
   eigenfunction of the diffusion generator with eigenvalue -1
   (EQ1, pure CAS); the four registered ensemble points sit inside
   their CLT bands on the curve. Figure: trajectories + decay.
2. LOCKED PHASE - the P-22 Z2 doublet read as a bit: a flip-flop
   priced from first principles. Lifetime = exact MFPT quadrature;
   Arrhenius barrier = eps EXACTLY by CAS; measured slope 0.970
   beside the pinned 0.976 with the CAS barrier 1. Figure: the
   double well, the telegraph, the Arrhenius line. (SRAM retention
   analysis uses precisely this Kramers structure - the modern bit
   is an engineered instance of this rung.)
3. WINDING NUMBER - the closed-form discovery of the line: clamp
   one bond at Delta and the relaxed remainder of the w = 1 sector
   is UNIFORM, so the whole unwinding path is analytic,
     E(Delta) = K(1-cos Delta) + K(N-1)(1-cos((2pi-Delta)/(N-1))),
   saddle at Delta* = pi(N-3)/(N-2), barrier saturating at 2K. The
   full Langer rate (Hessian determinants, zero mode dropped,
   unstable curvature, x N nucleation sites) was pinned for five
   (N, D) cells; escapes measured by the P-24a reset protocol land
   0.03-0.18 nat from the pins with 99.4-100 percent single-step
   purity. Figures: the ring states (w = 1, the saddle, w = 0),
   the unwinding path, the saturating barrier.

## What the line killed and what it caught

- The chat-level guess that 1D topological protection is extensive
  (lifetime ~ e^{cN/D}) died at EQ5, before registration: the
  barrier grows with N only because the twisted state's own strain
  relaxes, and saturates at 2K while nucleation contributes a
  factor N AGAINST persistence. The derived crossover: at D = 0.16
  a 32-ring outlives an 8-ring 128-fold; by D = 0.6 the ordering
  inverts (0.87). Measured at the budgeted cells: 19.2 vs derived
  23.6 (N 16 vs 8). This is the discrete-ring cousin of
  Langer-Ambegaokar-McCumber-Halperin: slip rate proportional to
  system size.
- N = 4 cannot hold the bit at all: the uniform w = 1 twist is
  pi/2 per bond - zero stiffness, barrier exactly zero. The figure
  page's barrier curve starts there deliberately.
- P-24a caught two protocol flaws before any run: an order clause
  naming an unbudgeted cell, and a free-running slip count that
  would have mixed the w = 0 sector (barrier near 2K) into rates
  priced for w = 1 escapes. Same class as P-22a; caught in time
  this time.

## Why this is the "fundamental, computational" answer

The week's audit arc kept finding that discreteness in real
substrates is EARNED - by locking, by topology, by order - never
primitive. This line makes that quantitative: the three ways a
classical substrate holds one distinguishable state, each with a
lifetime derived to the prefactor before measurement. The bit is a
readout convention on rungs whose physics is Adler wells and
winding saddles; the bit-ledger (MDL accounting) remains the
epistemic currency, and the mechanism stack is this. Follow-on
queued: the price of a bit - work-to-write against error rate on
rung 2, the Landauer-adjacent classical tradeoff (A-11).

Not claimed: quantum or 2D substrates, engineered error
correction, work/energy accounting (A-11), electron clouds
(declared out of scope for this classical line).
