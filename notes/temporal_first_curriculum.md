# A temporal-first geometry curriculum — design sketch

Mood: design note. The claim-shaped statements cited here (Malament;
Hawking-King-McCarthy; SI definitions; Sagnac) are classical and
citable; the curriculum itself is a proposal.

## The premise, stated honestly

Standard geometry pedagogy is space-first: points and lines as
primitives, time entering later as a parameter. But the foundations
of physics are ALREADY temporal-first, in three load-bearing ways:
(1) metrology — the SI meter is defined from the second via c, so
every length measurement is operationally a time measurement (radar
coordinates: d = c*Delta_t/2); (2) the causal-structure theorems —
Malament / Hawking-King-McCarthy: the temporal (causal) order of a
spacetime determines its topology and conformal geometry; add local
volume and you recover the full metric — i.e., ORDER IN TIME IS
SUFFICIENT PRIMITIVE DATA FOR GEOMETRY; (3) the audit record of the
parent corpus itself: every result that survived is temporal
machinery (locking, rotation numbers, staircases, Adler thresholds);
every fatality was a premature spatialization (d = 3, cells, lattice
counts). A temporal-first curriculum is therefore not exotic — it
teaches the actual dependency order and lets space be derived, which
is also the honest lesson of the P-1 spatial/temporal split.

## Modules (construct-then-name discipline, as in the v1 curriculum,
whose pedagogy survived audit even though its physics did not)

- **T0 — Return.** A process that repeats; counting returns. Number
  as counted recurrence, before any spatial object exists. Names
  earned: period, frequency.
- **T1 — Two rhythms.** Beats, drift, capture; the Adler threshold
  (already compendium C10). Ratio arises temporally: rationals are
  the lockable ratios; the golden ratio as the most stubborn
  drifter. Name earned: rotation number.
- **T2 — The first shape.** The circle, constructed as phase — the
  state space of one rhythm, time modulo period. Geometry's first
  object is a QUOTIENT OF TIME, not a locus of points.
- **T3 — Before and after.** Causal order as primitive; simultaneity
  as convention (clock synchronization); distance DEFINED by
  round-trip time. Anchor: this is how the SI actually defines the
  meter. Name earned: metric.
- **T4 — Desynchronization is curvature.** Clocks at different
  heights disagree; transport a clock around a loop and it returns
  offset (Sagnac; gravitational time dilation). Curvature introduced
  as the holonomy of clock transport — measured, in practice,
  temporally. Name earned: curvature, holonomy.
- **T5 — How many rhythms to address an event.** Dimension as the
  number of independent phases needed — stated honestly as partly
  open (the parent corpus's d = 3 route is declined; the question
  stands).
- **T6 — Journeys that return you inverted.** Antiperiodic return:
  period-doubling, the half-turn seam, the half-quantum lattice
  shift (the P-1 material: spectral half-integers, E1's shifted
  plateaus, Matsubara's odd frequencies). The Mobius/Klein material
  taught as a TEMPORAL phenomenon first — "the geometry remembers a
  reversal as a half-quantum" — with the spatial bottle derived
  afterward as its configuration-space shadow.
- **Capstone.** The causal-structure theorems as the payoff: from
  pure temporal order, recover topology and conformal geometry;
  discuss what must be ADDED (volume) and why — the curriculum's
  honest boundary, mirroring the framework's own two-anchor
  question.

## Why this belongs in this repo's orbit

The compendium already contains T1's engine (Adler), T2's objects
(staircase, rotation numbers), T4's germ (holonomy as the orientation
character), and T6's mathematics (the twisted spectrum, the gradient
XOR, E1). A temporal-first curriculum is largely an ARRANGEMENT of
already-verified material in dependency order — which is the only
kind of curriculum the gates would let us ship anyway.
